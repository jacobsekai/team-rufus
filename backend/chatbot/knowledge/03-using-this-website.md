# Using the T Levels at Amazon website

This describes the Team Rufus prototype website.

## You don't need an account to

- Read any guide or subject page.
- Download the Student checklist, Parent and carer guide, or Educator briefing.
- Register your interest in an Amazon placement.

## With a free account you can

- Save subjects and guides so you can find them again.
- Download resources for account holders, such as the placement preparation
  checklist.

## Creating an account

- You need your full name, an email address, and a password of 12 to 128
  characters that includes a number and a symbol.
- You must accept the Terms of use and read the Privacy notice.
- You can delete your account at any time from your account page. You will
  need to enter your password to confirm.

## Logging in

- Tick "Remember me" only on your own device. Without it, you are logged out
  when you close the browser.
- After 5 wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes.
- Forgot password is not available yet in this prototype.

## Registering your interest in an Amazon placement

- Students, parents and carers, schools and others can register.
- You choose which areas interest you: Digital, Business, Media, Finance or
  Engineering.
- You must agree that your details can be stored to contact you about
  placements.
- Registering interest is not an application for a placement.

## The chat assistant

- The "Ask about T Levels" button on every page opens the chat assistant.
- It answers questions about T Levels, placements and this website.
- Don't share personal details in the chat. Email addresses and phone numbers
  are hidden before your message reaches the assistant.
- The conversation is not saved. It disappears when you close or reload the
  page.

## Accessibility

- The Accessibility page lets you choose a larger text size and a high
  contrast or dark colour scheme.
- It also has text to speech. Turn it on there and a "Read page" button
  appears in the bottom-left corner of every page. It reads the page out
  loud and outlines the part being read. Select some text first to hear just
  that part. There are Pause and Stop buttons, the Escape key also stops, and
  the reading speed can be Slow, Normal or Fast. It uses the browser's own
  voice, so nothing is sent anywhere.
- You can also use your browser's zoom (Ctrl and + on Windows, Command and +
  on Mac).
