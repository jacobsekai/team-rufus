# T Level placements at Amazon

Sources: aboutamazon.co.uk (T Level placements page), checked 2026-10-20;
the project brief from the Amazon Emerging Talent Programs team; the Team
Rufus design (application stages). The application stages come from the
team's research and must be checked against current official guidance.

## What Amazon offers

- Amazon's official page describes Digital T Level placements for T Level
  students aged 16 to 18. It says Amazon plans to add other pathways,
  including creative, business and engineering.
- The Amazon Emerging Talent Programs team offers placements across Digital,
  Business, Media, Finance and Engineering (project brief).
- Placement availability changes. Never promise that a placement is
  available. Tell users to check Amazon's official careers channels and ask
  their school or college placement team.

## Application process (from the team's research)

1. Application: a written application or a short video answering three
   questions. Tip: use Amazon's Leadership Principles in your answers.
2. Group assessment: a welcome call, then an online group exercise. Tip: work
   with the other candidates, not against them.
3. Interview and presentation: an online interview and a short presentation
   you prepare in advance. Tip: practise your presentation so you know it well.

## Good to know

- A placement is part of your T Level course. It is not a job offer or an
  apprenticeship.
- Before you start, confirm travel, hours, supervision, accessibility and any
  age or safety rules with your school or college.
- Your school or college placement coordinator arranges placements with
  employers and can explain the process.

## Amazon's Leadership Principles

Amazon has 16 Leadership Principles, including Customer Obsession, Ownership,
Invent and Simplify, Learn and Be Curious, Bias for Action, Dive Deep and
Deliver Results. Applicants can use real examples from school, work or
hobbies that show these principles.
