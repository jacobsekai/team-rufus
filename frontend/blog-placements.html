Placements

What happens on an industry placement?

How long a T Level placement lasts, what you'll do, and what placements at Amazon look like.

The industry placement is what makes a T Level different. It's your chance to use what you've learned in a real workplace, with real colleagues.

How long is it?

At least 315 hours, which is about 45 days. Your school or college decides how it's spread out: in one block, as day release (for example one day a week), or a mix of both.

Do you get paid?

Not always. There's no legal requirement for T Level students to be paid on placement, so ask the employer or your college what to expect.

Placements at Amazon

Amazon offers Digital T Level placements for students aged 16 to 18. They run in 15-day stints at Amazon's skills hubs, and students are supported by buddies, mentors and placement managers. Schools and colleges contact Amazon to set them up.

Getting ready

Members can download our placement preparation checklist from the Information page. It covers your journey, who to contact and what to bring on your first day.
