The essentials

Good to know before you start.

Who can study a T Level?

T Levels are primarily for 16–19 year olds in England after GCSEs. Entry requirements are set by each school or college and may vary by course. Ask your provider about the grades and subjects you need.

How do I apply?

Choose a subject, find a provider and apply directly to that school or college. Check their deadlines, attend an open day and ask how they organise industry placements. You do not apply for a T Level through UCAS.

Is a T Level the same as an apprenticeship?

No. A T Level is a classroom-based course with an industry placement, split roughly 80% study and 20% placement. An apprenticeship is mostly paid work with some study. Both lead to skilled careers.

How long is a T Level?

A T Level takes two years to complete and is a Level 3 qualification, equivalent in size to three A Levels.

What can I do after a T Level?

You can move into skilled employment, a higher or degree apprenticeship, or university. T Levels carry UCAS points, so they are recognised by many higher education providers.
