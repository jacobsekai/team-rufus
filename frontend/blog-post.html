The essentials

Example Blog Page

What you study, how placements work and where a T Level could take you. A clear starting point for students and families in England.

Example blog body content. Replace this with the real article text once it is written. A T Level combines classroom study with a substantial industry placement, so you leave with both a recognised qualification and real workplace experience.

What you study

Each T Level focuses on one technical area, such as Digital, Business, Finance or Engineering. You build core knowledge first, then specialise in an occupational area that maps to real jobs.

How placements work

A T Level includes an industry placement of at least 45 days. Placements are arranged with employers in your area and give you a chance to apply what you have learned in a professional setting.

Where it could take you

T Levels are a Level 3 qualification, equivalent in size to three A Levels. They can lead to skilled employment, a higher apprenticeship, or university.
