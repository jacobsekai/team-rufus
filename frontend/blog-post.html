The essentials

T Levels: the essentials

What you study, how placements work and where a T Level could take you. A clear starting point for students and families in England.

A T Level is a 2-year course you can take after your GCSEs. It mixes classroom learning with a real industry placement, so you finish with a qualification and time spent in a workplace.

What you study

Each T Level focuses on one technical area, such as Digital, Business, Finance or Engineering. You start with core knowledge for the whole area, then choose an occupational specialism that links to real jobs.

How placements work

About 80% of your time is in the classroom and 20% is on an industry placement. The placement is at least 315 hours, which is about 45 days. It can be one block, a day a week, or a mix.

How you're graded

You get an overall grade of pass, merit, distinction or distinction*. If you haven't got grade 4 in English and maths yet, you keep working towards it, but you don't need it to pass.

Where it could take you

T Levels are a Level 3 qualification, equivalent in size to three A Levels. They can lead to a skilled job, a higher apprenticeship, or university.
