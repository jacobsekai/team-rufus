Application process

This is the application process for a T-Level Industry Placement at Amazon

Stage 1

Application: send a written application, or a short video answering three questions. Use Amazon's Leadership Principles in your answers.

Stage 2

Group assessment: join a welcome call, then an online group exercise. Work with the other candidates, not against them.

Stage 3

Interview and presentation: an online interview and a short presentation you prepare in advance. Practise it so you know it well.

LOCATIONS & PRACTICAL DETAILS

A placement that works for
you.

Amazon offers Digital T Level placements for students aged 16 to 18, and has said it plans to add creative, business and engineering T Levels. Before you start, check travel, hours, supervision and any accessibility needs with your school or college.

How to get ready

01

Know the Leadership Principles

Amazon has 16 Leadership Principles, such as Customer Obsession and Learn and Be Curious. Think of examples from school, work or hobbies that show them.

02

Talk to your school or college

Your school or college placement coordinator arranges placements with employers and can explain how the process works.
