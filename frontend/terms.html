Terms of use

The rules for using this website.

This website is a student prototype that uses test data only. It was built by Team Rufus for the Amazon Emerging Talent Programmes Team as part of a Digital T Level project. It isn't an official Amazon or government website.

Information on this website

  • We try to keep the information about T Levels and placements correct, but it may be out of date or wrong. Always check with TLevels.gov.uk and your school or college before making decisions.
  • Some pages and guides still contain placeholder content.

Your account

  • Use test details, not real ones. Keep your password to yourself.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your Account page.
  • We may remove accounts and data at any time. Everything is deleted when the prototype ends.

Registering your interest

Registering your interest isn't an application for a placement and doesn't guarantee one.

The chat assistant

  • The assistant is an AI and its answers can be wrong. Check anything important with an official source.
  • Don't share personal details in the chat.

Using the website fairly

  • Don't try to break into, overload or damage the website, or get at other people's accounts.
  • Don't use the chat assistant to produce harmful or offensive content.

Other websites

Links to other websites are there to help you. We're not responsible for their content.

Changes

We may change these terms while the prototype is being developed.
