Digital Software
Development

Turn an interest in technology into software that solves a real problem.
Explore practical programming, testing and thoughtful digital design.

← Back to Subjects

What will you learn to do?

Solve problems

Learn to break a problem into smaller steps and plan a solution before you start building it.

Write code

Programming is at the centre of the course. You write, test and improve software.

Work safely with data

Find out how data is stored and used, how to keep systems secure, and which laws apply.

What you could learn

The core topics are problem solving, programming, data, digital environments, security, business context, the laws and regulations for digital work, and emerging issues and the impact of digital. You then specialise in digital software development.

Projects that you may do

Each school or college plans its own projects, so ask the one you're interested in what you would work on and how it is assessed.

Classroom & Workplace

About 80% of your time is in the classroom and 20% is on an industry placement with an employer. The placement lasts at least 315 hours, which is about 45 days. It can be one block, a day a week, or a mix of both.

Entry requirements and assessment

Each school or college sets its own entry requirements, so check with the one you want to apply to. You get a grade for the core (A* to E), a grade for your specialism (pass, merit or distinction) and an overall grade (pass, merit, distinction or distinction*). If you don't have grade 4 in English and maths yet, you keep working towards it.

Where this area could lead

Jobs people go into include web developer, web designer, software developer, computer games tester or developer, e-learning developer and user experience (UX) designer. You could also go on to a related higher-level apprenticeship or a degree.

Before you apply

Check the college's deadlines, go to an open day and ask how they organise industry placements. If you're not quite ready for a T Level, ask about the T Level Foundation Year.
