For families

A quick guide for parents and carers

The key facts about T Levels for parents and carers, and how you can help.

If your child is thinking about a T Level, here are the basics to help you talk it through together.

What is a T Level?

A 2-year, Level 3 qualification taken after GCSEs, equivalent in size to three A Levels. It focuses on one technical area, such as Digital or Engineering.

How the time is split

About 80% classroom learning and 20% industry placement. The placement is at least 315 hours, about 45 days.

Is the placement paid?

There's no legal requirement for placements to be paid. Some employers choose to pay, so it's worth asking.

What it can lead to

A skilled job, a higher or degree apprenticeship, or a higher-level course or degree.
