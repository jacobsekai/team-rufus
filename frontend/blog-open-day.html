Choosing a course

Questions to ask at a T Level open day

Open days are the best time to find out if a course is right for you. Here's what to ask.

Every school and college runs its T Levels a little differently, so an open day is the best place to find out what yours will really be like.

About the course

Entry requirements are set by each school or college, so ask what they need from you. It's also worth asking which occupational specialisms they offer.

About placements

Ask how they find placements, which employers they work with, and whether your placement will be a block, day release or a mix.

If you need more support

If you haven't got grade 4 in English or maths, ask how they'll help you work towards it. If you're not ready for a T Level yet, ask whether they offer the T Level Foundation Year.
