Privacy notice

What this website stores about you, why, and how to see or delete it.

What we store

If you create an account

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your password, stored only as a scrambled hash. Nobody can read it back, including us.
  • When you accepted the Terms of use and this Privacy notice.
  • The subjects and guides you save.
  • How many wrong passwords have been entered in a row, so the account can be locked for 15 minutes after 5.
  • When your account was created and last changed.

When you log in

  • A random sign-in token in a cookie. We store only a scrambled copy of it. It ends when you log out, after 12 hours, or after 30 days if you tick “Remember me”.

If you register your interest in a placement

  • Your name, email address, who you are (student, parent or carer, school or college, or other), your school or college if you give it, the areas you're interested in, your agreement, and the date.

If you use the chat assistant

  • Your question is sent to Claude, an AI model, through Amazon Bedrock to get an answer. Email addresses and phone numbers are hidden before it gets there.
  • We don't save the conversation. It disappears when you close or reload the page. We only record how long each answer was, to keep an eye on costs.

Why we store it

  • To run your account, keep you logged in and protect it from password guessing.
  • To show your saved subjects and guides, and to let account holders download account-only guides.
  • To be able to contact you about T Level placements at Amazon, if you registered your interest and agreed to this.

Where it is kept

On Amazon Web Services in the United States: accounts, sign-ins and registrations in the Oregon region (us-west-2), and chat questions are answered in the North Virginia region (us-east-1). Access to the whole website needs an Amazon (Midway) sign-in.

How long we keep it

Sign-ins end as described above. Everything else is kept until you delete your account, or until the prototype is deleted after the project, whichever comes first.

Your choices

  • See and correct your details on your Account page.
  • Delete your account from your Account page. This removes your account and your saved items straight away.
  • Registrations of interest can't be viewed or changed on the website. To have one removed, ask the Team Rufus project team through the Amazon Emerging Talent Programmes Team.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Sign-in cookie: keeps you logged in. It can't be read by the website's JavaScript.
  • Midway cookies: Amazon's own sign-in, needed to open the prototype at all.
  • Display settings: your text size, colour contrast and text to speech choices are kept in your browser, not sent to us.
  • Your first name: kept in the browser tab while you're logged in, so the menu can show it straight away. It goes when you close the tab.

There are no advertising, tracking or analytics cookies.
