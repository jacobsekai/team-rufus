# Parent and carer guide
A short summary of T Levels for parents and carers of students aged 16 to 19.

## What is a T Level?
A T Level is a 2-year, Level 3 qualification taken after GCSEs. It is equivalent in size to 3 A levels. Each T Level focuses on one technical area, such as Digital, Business, Finance or Engineering.

## How the time is split
- About 80% of the course is in the classroom.
- About 20% is an industry placement with an employer.
- The placement is at least 315 hours (about 45 days). It can be a block, day release or a mix.

## Is the placement paid?
There is no legal requirement for T Level students to be paid on placement. Some employers choose to pay, so it's worth asking.

## English and maths
Students who haven't got grade 4 in English and maths work towards it during the course, but they don't need it to pass the T Level.

## What can it lead to?
- A skilled job
- A higher or degree apprenticeship
- A higher-level course or university degree

## Questions you might ask the college
- How will my child's placement be found and supported?
- Who is the contact at the college during the placement?
- How will we hear about progress?
