# Educator briefing
Conversation prompts and official links for teachers and careers advisers talking to students about T Levels.

## Key facts to share
- T Levels are 2-year, Level 3 courses, equivalent in size to 3 A levels.
- Around 80% classroom learning and 20% industry placement.
- Placements are at least 315 hours (about 45 days).
- Entry requirements are set by each school or college.
- Grades: overall pass, merit, distinction or distinction*.

## Conversation prompts
- Which subjects do you enjoy most, and why?
- Would you rather learn mainly through practical work or through exams and essays?
- How would you feel about spending time in a real workplace while you study?
- What jobs or areas of work are you curious about?

## Supporting students to choose
- Encourage them to visit open days and ask how placements are organised.
- Compare T Levels, A levels and apprenticeships side by side.
- Mention the T Level Foundation Year for students who need more preparation.
