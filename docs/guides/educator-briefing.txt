# Educator briefing
+ For teachers and careers advisers: the key facts about T Levels, some
+ questions to start the conversation, and where to point students next.

## The key facts
- T Levels are 2-year, Level 3 courses, the same size as 3 A levels.
- Students spend about 80% of their time in the classroom and 20% on an industry placement.
- The placement is at least 315 hours, which is about 45 days.
- Each school or college sets its own entry requirements.
- Students get an overall grade of pass, merit, distinction or distinction*.

## Starting the conversation
These questions help students think about whether a T Level suits the way they like to learn.
- Which subjects do you enjoy most, and what is it about them you like?
- Do you prefer hands-on, practical work, or exams and essays?
- How would you feel about spending time in a real workplace while you study?
- Are there any jobs or areas of work you're curious about?

## Helping students decide
- Encourage them to go to open days and ask how placements are organised.
- Look at T Levels, A levels and apprenticeships side by side, so they can see the differences.
- If a student isn't ready yet, the T Level Foundation Year could be a good first step.
