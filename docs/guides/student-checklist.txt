# Student checklist
Questions for open days and a simple plan for applying to a T Level.

## Is a T Level right for me?
[ ] I know which subject area interests me (Digital, Business, Finance, Engineering or another).
[ ] I'm happy to spend about 80% of my time in the classroom and 20% on an industry placement.
[ ] I understand the placement is at least 315 hours (about 45 days).
[ ] I've compared a T Level with A levels and apprenticeships.

## Questions to ask at an open day
[ ] What are your entry requirements for this T Level?
[ ] How do you find and organise industry placements?
[ ] Will my placement be a block, day release or a mix?
[ ] What support is there if I haven't got grade 4 in English or maths yet?
[ ] Do you offer the T Level Foundation Year if I'm not ready yet?
[ ] What have past students gone on to do?
