# Student checklist
+ Thinking about a T Level? Use this to work out if it's right for you,
+ what to ask at open days, and when to apply.

## Is a T Level right for me?
Tick the ones that sound like you. There are no wrong answers.
[ ] I have an idea of the area I'd like to work in, like Digital or Engineering.
[ ] I like the idea of learning in a classroom most of the time (about 80%) and spending the rest in a real workplace.
[ ] I'm up for a placement of at least 315 hours, which is about 45 days.
[ ] I've looked at A levels and apprenticeships too, so I can compare.

## Questions to ask at an open day
Don't be shy. Staff expect questions, and the answers will tell you a lot.
[ ] What do I need to get on to this course?
[ ] How do you find placements, and which employers do you work with?
[ ] Will my placement be in one block, one day a week, or a mix?
[ ] I haven't got grade 4 in English or maths yet. How would you help me?
[ ] If I'm not quite ready, do you run the T Level Foundation Year?
[ ] What did last year's students go on to do?
