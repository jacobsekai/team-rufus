# Placement preparation checklist
+ Your placement is coming up. Work through this so you can walk in on
+ day one feeling ready.

## Before your first day
[ ] I know the address and how I'm getting there, and I've done the journey once.
[ ] I know which days I'm in and my start and finish times.
[ ] I have my supervisor's name and how to contact them.
[ ] I know who to contact at my school or college while I'm on placement.
[ ] I know what to wear. If I'm not sure, I've asked.
[ ] If I need any support or adjustments, I've told my placement.

## What to bring
[ ] Lunch, or money for lunch
[ ] A notebook and pen
[ ] Any forms or documents my college asked me to bring

## Your first week
[ ] Find out who to go to with questions. Asking is normal, so ask.
[ ] Learn the health and safety rules and where the fire exits are.
[ ] Agree a few goals with your supervisor.

## If something goes wrong
- Running late or feeling ill? Let your supervisor know as early as you can.
- If you ever feel unsafe or unhappy, talk to your placement contact at college. That's what they're there for.
