# Placement preparation checklist
For account holders: things to sort out before your industry placement starts.

## Before your first day
[ ] I know the address and how I'll get there, and I've tried the journey.
[ ] I know my start time, finish time and which days I'm in.
[ ] I have the name and contact details of my placement supervisor.
[ ] I know who to contact at my school or college during the placement.
[ ] I know what to wear.
[ ] I've told my placement about anything I need support with.

## Things to bring
[ ] Lunch or money for lunch
[ ] Notebook and pen
[ ] Any documents or forms my college asked me to bring

## In your first week
[ ] Find out who to ask if I have a question.
[ ] Learn the health and safety rules and fire exits.
[ ] Agree my goals for the placement with my supervisor.

## If something goes wrong
